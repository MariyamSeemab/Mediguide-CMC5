# 🌿 Self-Care Tracker - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 How to Access

### Method 1: From Homepage
1. Start your development server: `npm run dev`
2. Open browser: `http://localhost:5173`
3. On the homepage, find the **"Self-Care Daily Tracker"** card
4. Click on it to open the Self-Care page

### Method 2: Direct URL
Navigate directly to: `http://localhost:5173/selfcare`

## 🎯 What You'll See

### 5 Main Tabs
1. **📊 Daily Tracker** - Track your daily health habits
2. **💡 Health Tips** - Get daily health tips
3. **🚑 First Aid** - Emergency first aid guides
4. **🌿 Home Remedies** - Traditional Indian remedies
5. **🧘 Exercises** - Breathing exercises and yoga

## 💡 Quick Features Tour

### Daily Tracker Tab
- **Mood**: Click on how you're feeling (😌 Calm, 🙂 Okay, 😟 Stressed, 😰 Very Stressed)
- **Water**: Use +/- buttons to track glasses of water
- **Steps**: Enter your daily step count
- **Sleep**: Drag slider to set sleep hours
- **Health Checks**: Check off daily habits
- **Eye Timer**: Click "Start Timer" for 20-second eye break
- **Notes**: Write about your day
- **Save**: Click "Save Today's Log" to save everything

### Health Tips Tab
- Read the featured "Tip of the Day"
- Browse 8 health tip cards
- All tips are practical and easy to follow

### First Aid Tab
- See emergency number: **108**
- Click on any emergency card to see step-by-step guide
- 6 emergencies covered: Cuts, Burns, Sprains, Nosebleed, Choking, Heart Attack

### Home Remedies Tab
- Click on any remedy card to see full details
- Get ingredients, method, and usage instructions
- 6 common ailments covered

### Exercises Tab
- Click "Try Now" on Breathing Exercise for guided practice
- Click "Try Now" on Yoga Poses to see 4 simple poses
- Browse other exercise cards for more ideas

## 🌍 Language Switching

1. Look for the language selector in the **top right** of the header

3. Select your preferred language
4. All content updates instantly!
