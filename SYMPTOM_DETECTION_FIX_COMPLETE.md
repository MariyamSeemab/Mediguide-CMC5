### Issue 2: "Leg is bleeding" - FIXED ✅

**New Category Added: INJURY & BLEEDING**

**Detection Keywords:**
- bleeding, blood, cut, wound, injury, injured, hurt, accident

**Immediate Actions Included:**
- Apply direct pressure with clean cloth
- Elevate injured area
- Do NOT remove embedded objects
- Keep pressure for 10-15 minutes
- Add more cloth if bleeding soaks through

**Emergency Detection:**
Enhanced emergency keywords to include:
- severe bleeding
- heavy bleeding
- bleeding heavily
- blood gushing
- won't stop bleeding

**Response Includes:**
- Immediate Actions for Bleeding
- Possible Medical Condition
- Recommended Specialist (Emergency care if severe)
- General Guidance (wound care)
- Common Medicines (pain relief, antiseptic)
- Basic Precautions (infection prevention)
- Emergency Warning (when to call 108)
- Doctor referral link
- Safety Note

**Classification:**
✅ Injury/Trauma
✅ First-aid guidance
✅ Emergency detection if severe bleeding

**Emergency Triggers:**
- Bleeding that won't stop after 15 minutes
- Deep or gaping wound
- Bleeding from head injury
- Object embedded in wound
- Signs of severe blood loss
- Animal or human bite

### Issue 3: "I am pregnant having pain" - FIXED ✅

**New Category Added: PREGNANCY WITH PAIN**

**Detection Keywords:**
- (pregnant OR pregnancy) AND (pain OR ache OR hurt OR cramp OR bleeding)

**Response Includes:**
- Important Note (pregnancy pain requires careful evaluation)
- Possible Medical Condition (round ligament pain, Braxton Hicks, serious conditions)
- Recommended Specialist (OB/GYN for immediate evaluation)
- General Guidance (rest, hydrate, monitor, contact OB/GYN)
- Common Medicines (DO NOT take ANY without OB approval)
- Basic Precautions (avoid self-medication, monitor fetal movement)
- Emergency Warning (comprehensive pregnancy emergencies)
- Doctor referral link
- Safety Note

**Classification:**
✅ Pregnancy-related concerns
✅ Women's health
✅ Pain severity assessment
✅ Emergency detection if severe symptoms

**Emergency Triggers:**
- Severe abdominal pain
- Vaginal bleeding (any amount)
- Fluid leaking from vagina
- Severe headache with vision changes
- Sudden swelling of face/hands/feet
- Decreased or no fetal movement
- Contractions before 37 weeks
- Fever with abdominal pain
- Dizziness or fainting
