# Chatbot Voice Features - User Guide

## For Rural Users / Non-Readers / Regional Language Speakers

### 🎧 How to Listen to Bot Responses

**Option 1: Auto-Speak (Automatic)**
1. Look at the top of the chatbot (header area)
2. Find the speaker icon with a toggle switch
3. Click to turn ON auto-speak
4. Now all bot responses will automatically read aloud!

**Option 2: Manual Play (Click to Listen)**
1. Bot sends a message
2. Look below the message
3. See small speaker icons (▶️ ⏸️ 🔄)
4. Click ▶️ (play) to hear the message
5. Click ⏸️ (pause) to pause
6. Click 🔄 (replay) to hear again

### 🎤 How to Speak Your Message

1. Look at the bottom input area
2. Find the microphone icon (🎤)
3. Click the microphone
4. It will turn orange and pulse (listening)
5. Speak your message in your language
6. It will automatically type what you said
7. Click send button or press Enter

### 🌐 How to Change Language

1. Look at the top right corner
2. Find the language dropdown (shows flag + language name)
3. Click to open dropdown
4. Select your language:
   - 🇮🇳 English
   - 🇮🇳 हिंदी (Hindi)
   - 🇮🇳 தமிழ் (Tamil)
   - 🇮🇳 తెలుగు (Telugu)
   - 🇮🇳 বাংলা (Bangla)
   - 🇮🇳 मराठी (Marathi)
   - 🇮🇳 ગુજરાતી (Gujarati)
   - 🇮🇳 ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)
   - 🇮🇳 മലയാളം (Malayalam)
   - 🇮🇳 ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi)
   - 🇮🇳 ଓଡ଼ିଆ (Odia)
   - 🇮🇳 অসমীয়া (Assamese)
   - 🇮🇳 اردو (Urdu)
4. Everything changes to your language automatically!

### 🔊 Sounds You'll Hear

**Opening Sound** (when chatbot opens)
- Soft ascending tones
- Means: "Chatbot is ready!"

**Response Sound** (when bot replies)
- Gentle notification
- Means: "New message from bot"

**Success Sound** (when mic understands you)
- Quick ascending tones
- Means: "Got it! Your speech was recognized"

## Complete Workflow Example

### Example 1: User Who Cannot Read (Hindi Speaker)

1. **Open chatbot** → Hear opening sound
2. **Change language** → Select "हिंदी"
3. **Turn on auto-speak** → Toggle speaker icon
4. **Click microphone** → Speak: "मुझे सिरदर्द है"
5. **Bot responds** → Automatically reads: "मुझे बताइए, सिरदर्द कब से है?"
6. **Click microphone again** → Speak: "दो दिन से"
7. **Bot responds** → Automatically reads advice in Hindi

### Example 2: User Who Prefers Listening (Tamil Speaker)

1. **Open chatbot** → Hear opening sound
2. **Change language** → Select "தமிழ்"
3. **Type message** → "காய்ச்சல் இருக்கிறது"
4. **Bot responds** → See message
5. **Click play button** (▶️) → Hear response in Tamil
6. **Want to hear again?** → Click replay button (🔄)

### Example 3: User Who Wants Both (Telugu Speaker)

1. **Open chatbot** → Hear opening sound
2. **Change language** → Select "తెలుగు"
3. **Turn on auto-speak** → Toggle speaker icon
4. **Click microphone** → Speak in Telugu
5. **Bot responds** → Automatically reads in Telugu
6. **Want to pause?** → Click pause button (⏸️)
7. **Want to continue?** → Click play button (▶️)

## Tips for Best Experience

### For Speaking (Microphone):
- ✅ Speak clearly and slowly
- ✅ Speak in your selected language
- ✅ Wait for orange pulse before speaking
- ✅ Speak one sentence at a time
- ❌ Don't speak too fast
- ❌ Don't speak in noisy environment

### For Listening (Speaker):
- ✅ Use headphones for better sound
- ✅ Adjust device volume if needed
- ✅ Can pause and resume anytime
- ✅ Can replay as many times as needed
- ❌ Don't play multiple messages at once

### For Language:
- ✅ Select your language first
- ✅ Mic will understand that language
- ✅ Speaker will read in that language
- ✅ Can change language anytime

## Troubleshooting

### Microphone Not Working?
1. Check if browser asks for permission
2. Click "Allow" when asked
3. Check device microphone is working
4. Try Chrome or Edge browser (best support)

### Speaker Not Working?
1. Check device volume is not muted
2. Check browser allows sound
3. Try clicking play button again
4. Try different browser

### Wrong Language?
1. Check language dropdown at top
2. Select correct language
3. Mic and speaker will update automatically

### Sounds Too Loud/Soft?
1. Adjust device volume
2. Sounds are designed to be soft
3. Speech volume can be adjusted in device settings

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Esc** → Stop speaking (if playing)
