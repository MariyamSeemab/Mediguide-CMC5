## 📖 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### STEP 1: Set Up Your Cycle (First Time)

1. Click on **"📝 Period Tracker"** tab
2. **Set Last Period Start Date**:
   - Click the date input field
   - Select the first day of your last period
   - It saves automatically!
3. **Adjust Cycle Length** (optional):
   - Default is 28 days
   - Change if your cycle is different (21-35 days)
4. **Adjust Period Duration** (optional):
   - Default is 5 days
   - Change to match your typical period length (3-7 days)

✅ You'll immediately see predictions for:
- 🩸 Next Period Date
- 🥚 Ovulation Date

---

### STEP 2: Log Your Daily Data

**Every day, go to "📝 Period Tracker" tab:**

1. **Select Your Mood** (Click one):
   - 😊 Happy
   - 😐 Neutral
   - 😢 Sad
   - 😰 Anxious
   - 😠 Angry

2. **Track Water Intake**:
   - Click **+** button to add glasses
   - Click **-** button to remove
   - Goal: 8-10 glasses per day
   - (Water helps reduce bloating and cramps!)

3. **Select Symptoms** (Click all that apply):
   - Cramps
   - Headache
   - Bloating
   - Mood Swings
   - Fatigue
   - Back Pain
   - Acne
   - Cravings

4. **Select Flow Intensity** (if on period):
   - Light
   - Medium
   - Heavy
   - None (if not on period)

5. **Add Notes** (optional):
   - Type anything you want to remember
   - Example: "Felt great after yoga" or "Bad cramps in morning"

6. **Click "💾 Save Today's Log" button**
   - You'll see a success message
   - Green checkmark ✅ appears next to the date

---

### STEP 3: Use the Calendar

**Go to "📅 Calendar" tab:**

**What You'll See:**
- 🩸 **Pink days** = Your period (predicted)
- 🌱 **Green days** = Fertile window (5 days before ovulation)
- 🥚 **Yellow day** = Ovulation day
- ✅ **Green dot** = Days you've logged data
- **Bold border** = Today

**How to Use:**
1. Click **‹** or **›** to change months
2. Click **any day** to log data for that specific date
3. A popup opens where you can:
   - Log mood
   - Track flow
   - Log water intake
   - Select symptoms
   - Add notes
   - Click "Save" button

**Pro Tip:** Log past days if you forgot!

## 🎯 Common Questions

**Q: Why isn't the calendar showing colors?**
A: You need to set your "Last Period Start Date" in the Period Tracker tab first!

**Q: Why don't I see any analytics?**
A: You need to log at least one day of data. Go to Period Tracker and save today's log!

**Q: Can I edit past days?**
A: Yes! Go to Calendar tab and click any day to log or edit data for that date.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes! All data is saved locally on YOUR device only. Nothing is sent to any server.

**Q: What if I miss a day?**
A: No problem! Use the calendar to go back and log that day whenever you remember.

**Q: How accurate are the predictions?**
A: Predictions are based on your cycle length. They become more accurate as you log more cycles.
